What changed on the back & why

The back now earns revenue without sacrificing brand identity or USPS compliance. Premium spots are differentiated enough from standard spots to justify 3x the price — not just "a spot on the back."

1

Premium tag

Blue "Featured" tag in the corner marks these as higher-tier placements. Signals status to the household and justifies pricing to the advertiser.

2

Editorial treatment

Hero spots use italic serif body copy ("family-owned since 2004") instead of the coupon-style offer format. Reads as a story, not a coupon.

3

Big offer panel

Right side of each hero spot is a bold offer block — $500 OFF, 8 WK TURNKEY. Visible from across the room. This is the visual hook.

4

Network promo preserved

Middle-right still has the "your business here?" recruitment block. Growth flywheel intact — every household still gets shown how to become advertiser #11.

5

USPS zones untouched

Indicia, return address, and Postal Customer block all in the exact positions USPS requires. Counter staff accepts on first review. No compliance risk.

6

Brand strip carries over

Same logo + issue number treatment as front. Reinforces "this is Darksteel Mail" as a recognizable monthly piece, not a one-off flyer.
